Gamma does not replace fraud detection, AML screening, compliance or policy management. Those systems define controls and produce authoritative signals.
Gamma evaluates the approved predicate manifest, binds the result to one exact action, and releases execution only when authorization, evidence commitment and runtime health all concur.
Every PERMIT or SAFE_STATE decision is sealed into tamper-evident, independently replayable authorization evidence.
