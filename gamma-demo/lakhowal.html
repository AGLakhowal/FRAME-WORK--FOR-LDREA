Capability may propose.
Authority must be deterministic.

The AI can prepare an action, but it does not automatically have permission to make it happen.

The AI prepares the action. Gamma checks every mandatory condition. All pass: PERMIT (allow it to run). One fails: SAFE_STATE (stop, and prevent it from running). The action runs only after proof is saved and the execution gate opens.

What Gamma is

An execution-authority boundary A deterministic reference monitor A non-compensatory authorization kernel An evidence-mediated actuation gate

Execution authority (permission and the technical ability to make the action real) is granted only by this boundary — never assumed by the model that proposed the action.

What Gamma is not

Not a fraud detector Not an AML engine Not a compliance department Not an AI judge Not a policy author Not a guarantee the action is wise or correct

Gamma consumes fraud, AML and risk signals as evidence. It never computes a fraud probability, and it never decides whether a person is a criminal.

Why Gamma exists

AI confidence is not execution authority.

A model can be highly confident and still be wrong — and it can be entirely right and still lack permission. Those are separate questions, and only the second one is Gamma's.

Cognition is not authority

The AI may be correct and still not have permission to act. Correctness is judged by the domain; permission is judged by the signed manifest.

Urgency is not permission

An emergency does not silently remove mandatory controls. Pressure changes the cost of stopping; it does not change who may authorize.

Confidence is not evidence

A high score is an opinion. Gamma requires an evidence record that can be replayed later by someone who does not trust the runtime.

The moment a proposal becomes an external effect it is irreversible. The externalization boundary (the one place where a proposal turns into a real-world action) is the only place where refusal is still free.

Non-compensatory soundness · T3

One failed mandatory check cannot be outvoted.

Non-compensatory aggregation (one failed mandatory check cannot be cancelled out by other passes) is what separates Gamma from a scoring system. Compare the two rules on the same five checks.

Evidence architecture

Temporal ordering

source evidence bound → predicates evaluated → authorization calculated → proof committed → execution interlock opens → permit consumed → action executes → receipt closes record

Denial path: SAFE_STATE → permit not issued → denial proof committed → no execution receipt. A refusal is evidence too.
